Printable Listing Worksheet

Print this page and fill it out by hand before you list. It keeps your item details in one place so you can paste them into AI prompts and your eBay listing quickly.

Press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) to print this worksheet.

How to use it

  1. Print this page (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P).
  2. Fill in each row as you prep the listing.
  3. Use the details in the AI prompts from the full manual — swap the bracketed values for your real answers.
  4. Paste the final title, description, and policy into eBay.

Keep completed worksheets in a folder so you can look up past sales and prices later.
