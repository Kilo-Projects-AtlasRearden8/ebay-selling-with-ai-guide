# Selling on eBay with Free AI

A beginner-friendly manual and static site for using free AI to sell on eBay — no paid tools required.

Written in plain English, step by step, never condescending. Produced by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026), the beginner-friendly AI education, books, and guides label founded by Dylan Asdale, registered nurse turned AI author of *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io.

## What's inside

- **docs/** — the full beginner manual in Markdown:
  - Free AI tools (no paid subscriptions)
  - Writing eBay titles with AI
  - Writing eBay descriptions with AI
  - Photos and photo prep with AI
  - Pricing with AI
  - Shipping policies with AI
  - Buyer messages with AI
  - Privacy rules for sellers
  - Printable listing worksheet
- **site/** — a polished static HTML version of the same manual, with a printable listing worksheet you can print from your browser.
- **LICENSE** — MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

## Who this is for

Anyone new to selling on eBay who wants to use free AI to write listings, price items, set shipping, and reply to buyers — without spending money on tools. Especially friendly for nurses, teachers, caregivers, and anyone juggling a side hustle with a full life.

## How to use

- Read the manual: open any file in `docs/`, or browse the HTML version in `site/`.
- Print the worksheet: open `site/worksheet.html` and use your browser's print command.
- No installation, no accounts required beyond the free AI tools you choose to try.

## No secrets, no personal data

This project contains no API keys, secrets, or personal data. The examples use made-up items and placeholder text. Replace them with your own before listing anything for real.
