# 1. Free AI tools (no paid tools needed)

You can run this entire eBay AI workflow with free tools. You do not need a subscription.

## Free options to try

| Tool | Cost | Best for |
|------|------|----------|
| Google AI Studio (Gemini) | Free tier | Titles, descriptions, pricing research, messages |
| Bing Chat / Copilot | Free | Descriptions, shipping policy drafts, buyer replies |
| Poe | Free tier (daily limits) | Trying several models in one place |
| Local AI (Ollama + a small model) | Free, runs on your computer | Maximum privacy — nothing leaves your machine |

Pick one to start. Google AI Studio and Bing Copilot work in your browser with no install. If you want privacy first, try Ollama on your own computer (see the local AI guide in the Asdale Dynamics library).

## How to use a free AI tool for eBay

1. Open the tool in your browser.
2. Copy one of the prompts from this manual.
3. Paste it in, fill in the bracketed details with your item, and send.
4. Read the result carefully. Fix anything inaccurate.
5. Copy the cleaned-up text into your eBay listing.

## A sample first prompt

Use this to test that the tool works:

> I'm selling a used [item name, e.g. men's Nike running shoes, size 10] on eBay. The condition is [excellent/good/fair] and it has [any flaws, e.g. slight scuff on left heel]. Write a friendly, honest eBay title (80 characters or less) and a short description (about 3 short paragraphs) that mentions the brand, size, condition, and what's included. Do not make up facts.

Save the prompts you like in a notes app so you can reuse them.

## What AI is good at (and not good at)

**Good at:** Drafting titles, descriptions, policies, and messages; organizing details; suggesting search terms.

**Not good at:** Knowing your item's true condition, knowing exact sold prices, guaranteeing a sale, handling money or shipping labels. Always double-check AI output against your real item and eBay's current rules.
