# Selling on eBay with Free AI — Beginner Manual

Welcome. This manual walks you through using **free AI** to sell on eBay: writing titles, descriptions, prepping photos, pricing, shipping policies, and replying to buyers. No paid tools required.

It is written by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and guides founded by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author. The tone is plain English, step by step, never condescending. Think of it as a friend showing you the ropes.

## The big idea

You don't need to be technical, and you don't need to pay for AI. Free AI tools can write a first draft of your title, description, policies, and messages. You then read it, fix anything wrong, and add your own voice. AI is the assistant — you're still the seller in charge.

## A quick safety reminder before you start

Never put your real name, address, phone number, email, bank details, Social Security number, or eBay password into a free AI tool. See the [Privacy rules](08-privacy.md) section for the full list.
