# 3. Writing eBay descriptions with AI

The description builds trust. A good one tells the buyer exactly what they're getting, so there are fewer questions and fewer returns.

## What a good eBay description includes

- What the item is (brand, model, size, color)
- Condition and any flaws (be honest — photos should match)
- What's included in the sale
- Measurements or specs, if relevant
- Shipping speed and carrier
- Return policy (if you offer returns)
- A short, friendly closing

## Free AI prompt for descriptions

> I'm selling [item name] on eBay. Brand: [brand]. Model/size: [size]. Color: [color]. Condition: [condition], and here are the flaws: [flaws or "none visible"]. Includes: [what's included]. I ship within [1–3] business days via [carrier]. Returns: [accepted/not accepted, within X days]. Write a warm, honest eBay description in about 4 short paragraphs. Use short sentences and bullet lists where helpful. Do not invent features or guarantees.

## Example: a used coffee maker

> I'm selling a coffee maker on eBay. Brand: Keurig. Model: K-Classic. Condition: good, works perfectly, minor scuff on the base. Includes: coffee maker and power cord, no pod holder. I ship within 1 business day via USPS. Returns accepted within 30 days, buyer pays return shipping...

The AI turns that into a clear, scannable listing. You read it, fix anything wrong, and paste it into eBay.

## Tips

- Use bullet points for condition and what's included — buyers skim.
- Mention flaws up front; honest listings get fewer disputes.
- Never copy a manufacturer's marketing claims unless you can verify them.
- Keep the tone friendly but professional.
