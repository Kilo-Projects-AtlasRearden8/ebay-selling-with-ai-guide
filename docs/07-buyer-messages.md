# 7. Buyer messages with AI

Quick, polite replies keep buyers happy and protect your seller rating. AI can draft replies for common situations.

## Common buyer messages and AI prompts

**Asking a question before buying:**
> A buyer is asking: "[their question]." The item is [item], [condition], [price], ships within [X] days via [carrier]. Write a short, friendly eBay reply answering the question and inviting them to buy. Don't make up facts.

**After purchase (thank you + ship date):**
> Write a friendly eBay message to a buyer who just bought [item]. Thank them, confirm it will ship within [X] business days via [carrier], and say they'll get a tracking number. Keep it under 4 sentences.

**Shipping delay:**
> Write a polite eBay message telling a buyer their item will ship [X] days later than expected because [reason]. Apologize briefly, give the new ship date, and thank them for patience. No over-explaining.

**Return request:**
> A buyer wants to return [item] because [reason]. My policy is [your policy]. Write a courteous eBay reply that explains next steps and follows my policy. Stay calm and professional.

**Negative or upset buyer:**
> A buyer is upset because [issue]. Write a calm, professional eBay reply that acknowledges their concern, offers a fair next step, and avoids arguing. Do not admit fault unless it's true.

## Message tips

- Reply within 24 hours when you can.
- Keep it short and warm.
- Never share personal info (phone, address, email) — use eBay's messaging system.
- If a buyer gets rude or threatening, contact eBay instead of arguing.
