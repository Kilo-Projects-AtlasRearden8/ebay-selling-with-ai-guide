# 8. Privacy rules for eBay sellers using AI

Free AI tools are helpful, but you must protect your personal information and your buyers' information. Treat every AI prompt as if it could be read by others.

## Never put these into a free AI tool

- Your real name, home address, phone number, or email
- eBay username or password
- Bank account, credit card, or PayPal details
- Social Security number or tax ID
- Buyer names, addresses, or order details
- Photos showing your face, your home, mail, kids, or anything identifying

## Safe practice: use placeholders

Instead of your real details, use brackets:

> I'm selling [item], [brand], [condition]. I ship within [X] days via [carrier]. Returns [accepted/not accepted].

Fill in the real details only when you paste the final text into eBay — never into the AI tool.

## If you want maximum privacy

Use a local AI tool (e.g., Ollama) that runs on your own computer. Nothing leaves your machine. This is the best choice if you're nervous about cloud tools.

## Remember: AI output isn't private either

Don't assume your conversations with a free AI tool are secret. Avoid sharing anything you wouldn't want posted publicly.

## Buyer data

If a buyer messages you on eBay, reply through eBay's messaging system. Don't copy their address or personal details into an AI tool to draft a reply — use placeholders instead.

Stay safe, and happy selling.
