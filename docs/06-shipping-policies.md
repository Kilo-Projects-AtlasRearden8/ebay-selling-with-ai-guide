# 6. Shipping policies with AI

Your shipping policy tells buyers how fast you ship, which carrier you use, and whether you accept returns. Clear policies reduce questions and disputes.

## What to decide before listing

- Handling time (how soon you ship after purchase, e.g., 1 business day)
- Carrier (USPS, UPS, FedEx) and service (Ground, Priority, etc.)
- Who pays return shipping, if you accept returns
- Whether you offer free shipping or charge the buyer

## Free AI prompt for shipping policy

> I sell on eBay. I ship within [1–3] business days via [USPS/UPS/FedEx]. I [do/do not] offer free shipping. Returns: [accepted within X days, buyer pays return shipping / not accepted]. Write a short, friendly eBay shipping and return policy in 2–3 short paragraphs. Be clear and honest. Do not promise anything I can't deliver.

## Example: USPS, 1-day handling, returns accepted

> I ship within 1 business day of payment via USPS. Items are packed securely and shipped with tracking when available. Returns are accepted within 30 days; the buyer pays return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or not as described. Message me with any questions before purchasing.

Copy that into your eBay shipping policy or listing description, then adjust to match what you actually do.

## Tips

- Only promise handling times you can keep (nurses and shift workers — be realistic about your schedule).
- Use tracking for anything over ~$25.
- Take a photo of the packed item and the shipping label receipt in case of a dispute.
